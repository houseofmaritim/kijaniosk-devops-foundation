# Reflection

## Where were you tempted to take shortcuts?
I was tempted to skip detailed explanations for IAM and network design, but I ensured each decision was clearly explained to reflect proper DevOps thinking.

## Which architectural decision required the most reasoning?
The network segmentation required the most reasoning, especially deciding how to separate public and private subnets while maintaining secure access.

## If the KijaniKiosk platform grows significantly, what would you improve first?
I would introduce auto-scaling, monitoring, and CI/CD pipelines to improve scalability, performance, and reliability.
